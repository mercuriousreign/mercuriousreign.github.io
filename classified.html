Pipeline to creativity

Many have tried to make a story that is in motion, They are manifested in either live action, 3d animation, 2d animation. They all come to fruition through complex production and involve huge amounts of man power.
But there are other ways where the right tool, the right aesthetic and the right community forms around the base point to create an unique environment that fosters Storytelling in motion. THis is a list of examples where such storytelling took place. They can be categorized into 2 things, Machinimas and Media Editing.

  • Machinimas
  • Media Editing
  • Others

  1. Machinimas

    User utilizes the engine and video games capabilities to act out a story in real time Examples: Red vs Blue

  2. Media Editing:

    User rips out and uses the content of a video game to remix them in a content creation software for storytelling purposes
    Examples: Kerning City's Dark Lord Awakens | Global MapleStory Classic World

  3. Others

    Note sometimes an software can encompasess all the tools in one place to foster similar environment, they are very similar to traditional pipelines such a blender but the software is built in such a way that user doesnt have to leave the software itself
    Examples: Katashi,

Note: Chronicles of M is a media project that utilizes everything media related to tell a story and explore Chronicles of M
